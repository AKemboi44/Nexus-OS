Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how Nexus Research AI (“Nexus,” “we,” or “our”) handles information when you use the website and Chrome extension. Nexus Research AI is operated under the Brisklight AI brand; privacy questions can be sent to brisklightke@gmail.com.

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and authentication identifiers from Supabase Auth.
  • Research content: topics, chosen evidence criteria, source metadata, and files you choose to upload for analysis.
  • Saved work: research results, saved dossier indexes, saved sources, and generated workbook files associated with your account.
  • Support and billing information: Research Pack purchases, remaining scans, expiry dates, and PayPal order and transaction identifiers needed to operate paid features and handle refunds. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider, not stored by the extension.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, run requested research scans and reports, save and retrieve your work, enforce service limits and pack balances, maintain system security, and understand service reliability. Research topics and sources are sent to our API and the relevant research or AI providers used to fulfill your request.

Service providers

Nexus uses Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage; Railway for API hosting; PayPal for pack checkout; and external scholarly or AI services to provide requested research features. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Chrome extension permissions

The extension uses local storage to keep your sign-in session and scan cache. Generated reports may be cached locally in IndexedDB for up to 30 days, subject to a per-report size limit. Chrome Identity starts Google sign-in, and the Side Panel hosts the research workspace. The extension requests network access to the Nexus API and Supabase. It does not read browsing history or inject scripts into visited pages.

Retention and deletion

Saved research and associated account records are retained to provide your account history. Generated dossier workbooks are stored in private storage. To request access to or deletion of your data, email brisklightke@gmail.com from your account email address. Payment records may be kept as long as needed for accounting and dispute handling.

Security

We use authenticated API requests and owner-scoped access to protect saved research. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and sign out on shared devices.

Children and changes

Nexus is a research productivity service and is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the product changes and will update the date above when we do.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, email brisklightke@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +254 743 852 707. Please do not send passwords or payment card details in a chat.
