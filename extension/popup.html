Nexus Research AI Terms and Conditions

These terms explain how the research workspace, accounts, subscriptions, payments, and generated outputs are handled.

1. Account registration

You must provide accurate account information and keep your sign-in details secure. Each account is intended for the person or organization that created it.

2. Research workspace

Nexus Research AI provides evidence discovery, source auditing, synthesis, and report-generation tools. You are responsible for reviewing sources and deciding how to use generated material.

3. Payments and subscriptions

Paid plans are billed at the price shown at checkout and renew according to the selected payment provider's schedule. Payment credentials are handled by the provider and are not stored in the extension.

4. Refunds and cancellation

Refund requests should normally be submitted within seven days of a charge. Completed scans, generated reports, and used subscription time may be excluded. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. Future renewals may be cancelled through the payment provider.

5. Research-output policy

Research outputs are decision-support aids. They are not guarantees of truth, safety, publication acceptance, legal compliance, or professional advice. Verify sources, interpretations, and conclusions independently.

6. Acceptable use

Do not use the service for unlawful activity, abuse of third-party systems, unauthorized access, or attempts to bypass usage, payment, or security controls.

7. Changes

We may update these terms as the service develops. Material changes will be communicated through the product or the account contact information where appropriate.
