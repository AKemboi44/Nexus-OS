## Full description

Nexus Research AI helps you move from a research question to a structured evidence review.

- Discover scholarly sources and review inclusion and exclusion decisions.
- Inspect source metadata and evidence summaries before using results.
- Generate a downloadable Excel audit dossier and Word research reports.
- Sign in securely and access your saved research from the Nexus website.

Research outputs are starting points for independent review, not guarantees of accuracy or professional advice. Verify citations and conclusions before relying on them.

## Permission declarations

- **Storage:** Keeps the user's Supabase authentication session and local scan cache.
- **Identity:** Starts Google OAuth using Chrome's identity flow.
- **Side panel:** Hosts the research workspace.
- **Host access:** Calls the Nexus Railway API and Supabase Auth for signed-in research and account features.

The extension does not inject scripts into arbitrary websites or read browsing history.
